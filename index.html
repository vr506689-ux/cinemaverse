About Cinemaverse

A free, open-source virtual cinema you play in the browser. This is Phase 1: the 3D lobby and auditorium, a walkable character, camera and collisions.

Coming next: richer cinema detail (Phase 2), multiplayer rooms (Phase 3), synced video on the big screen (Phase 4), chat and voice (Phase 5), accounts and friends (Phase 6), phone controls (Phase 7).

The film posters show made-up titles. Everything in the 3D world is generated by code, so there are no third-party model or texture licences to worry about. Built with Three.js (MIT licence).

This browser can't run the 3D world
